Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Friend Draft ("we", "our", "the app") is built to be simple, fun, and private. Here's exactly what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Friend names you enter — stored only on your device during gameplay. Never sent to our servers. Never stored after you close the app.
  • Anonymous game events — e.g. "a game was played", "a scenario was picked", "a result was shared". No names, no device IDs. Used only to understand which scenarios are popular.
  • Purchase receipts — handled entirely by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to verify your subscription status. We never see your payment details.
  • Push notification token — stored locally on your device and optionally on our servers to send you challenge notifications. You can disable this at any time in iOS/Android settings.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, email, or any account information (no account required)
  • Location data
  • Photos or camera data (the app saves result cards to your own Photos library only)
  • Contacts
  • Advertising identifiers

Third-party services

  • RevenueCat — processes in-app subscription purchases. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — stores anonymous analytics events. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS — used to build and deliver app updates. Expo Privacy Policy

Data retention

Anonymous analytics events are retained for 12 months and then deleted. Push notification tokens are deleted if you uninstall the app or revoke notification permissions.

Children

Friend Draft is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

Your rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to request deletion of. If you have questions, email us at privacy@frienddraft.app.

Changes

If we ever change this policy, we'll update the date above and note it in the app's release notes.
